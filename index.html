§ 06

You asked.
We answered.

The questions that come up most in our DMs.

Is SkillShare Hub actually free? +

Yes — sign-up, browsing, and messaging are all free. Tutors may set a price for sessions, but many trade freely. You can also barter skill-for-skill.

Who can join? +

Any student with a verified campus email. We check institutional domains to keep the community trusted.

How do I book a session? +

Find a tutor, hit “Request,” pick a time, send a message. They'll confirm or suggest an alternative. Done.

Can I teach AND learn? +

Absolutely. Most users do both. It's the whole point — a two-way street of campus talent.

How do ratings work? +

After every session, students leave a star rating + written note. Tutors' averages surface on every listing.

What if I need to cancel? +

Cancel any session from your dashboard. We ask for 24h notice as a courtesy to your tutor.
